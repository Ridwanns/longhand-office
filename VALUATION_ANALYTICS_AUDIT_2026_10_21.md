# Valuation input and output review — 21 October 2026

The automatic US/IDX valuation now separates five compact source measures from three assumption-based scenario cards. A native dot comparison uses one shared price axis and the dated source price as a reference. The cards show assumed growth, exit P/E, annual price return, discounted entry, entry gap and the EPS growth needed to reach the supplied required return. These are mechanical sensitivities, not independent price targets or calibrated probabilities. Missing/mismatched EPS does not generate a chart or a zero estimate.

Required EPS growth solves `EPS0 × (1 + growth)^N × exitPE = sourcePrice × (1 + requiredReturn)^N`. Equivalently, `(1 + requiredReturn) × exp((log(currentPE) − log(exitPE)) / N) − 1`. This uses the same per-share earnings, currency, horizon, exit multiple and zero-dividend assumption as the original scenario. No FX, additional dilution, independently estimated exit multiple or dividend forecast is inferred.

Eligible automatic DCF and the manual IDR/USD FCFE tools add a decomposition of forecast PV, terminal PV and total equity value, the discount/growth spread, and a 25-case cost-of-equity/terminal-growth sensitivity matrix. Annual forecast cash flows stay fixed in that matrix. Discount rates vary by ±1/2 percentage points; terminal growth by ±0.5/1 percentage points. Invalid engine cases stay unavailable. This is a local sensitivity study, not a confidence interval. Negative forecast PV and terminal contributions above 100% remain visible. Diagram coordinates are normalized before subtraction, preserving finite extreme-scale inputs without visual overflow.

Manual FCFE input now selects one explicit route: annual amounts or base/growth/horizon. Annual amounts require one unformatted number per line, with dot decimals (finite scientific notation also supported). Commas, grouping separators and missing intermediate years are rejected rather than interpreted as different annual cash flows. Inactive fields are disabled and excluded from the canonical input. A visible preview shows the year count and first/last amounts. New and legacy base-growth drafts restore their correct active route. Old comma-delimited draft text is preserved for explicit correction; canonical calculation backups still restore numeric arrays directly.

The existing USD, IDR and automatic valuation engine versions, saved result structures, source records and storage identifiers remain unchanged. Derived charts are regenerated from retained inputs. Display rounding does not replace full-precision archived results. The duplicate manual year-by-year breakdown is replaced by the cash-flow chart and its observation table for successful FCFE calculations. Unavailable calculations keep their original evidence. The local server and static Pages build both include the new modules; the import graph is cache-versioned.
