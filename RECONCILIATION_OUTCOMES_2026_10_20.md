# Source coverage, portfolio reconciliation and prospective outcomes

This continues the approved priorities 1–3. It adds a real connected source and accounting/review workflows; it does not claim that every missing provider series is now available or that predictive ability has been established.

## Public source improvement

Australia's reported monthly all-groups CPI annual rate is connected to the [Reserve Bank of Australia G4 CSV](https://www.rba.gov.au/statistics/tables/csv/g4-data.csv), sourced by RBA to the Australian Bureau of Statistics. The parser checks the table identity, GCPIAGYPM code, monthly/original basis, ABS source, per-cent unit, month-end dates and publication vintage. A UTF-8 byte-order marker and legitimately ragged rows are handled without relaxing other collectors' rectangular CSV checks. Missing periods remain missing. The table's publication date is retained as its update vintage, not invented as each historical observation's original release date.

The connected collection has short monthly history and is not spliced to quarterly CPI or the discontinued monthly indicator. ABS documents the [monthly collection and its history](https://www.abs.gov.au/statistics/economy/price-indexes-and-inflation/consumer-price-index-australia/latest-release). GEM's unavailable Australia series remains unavailable; source selection can use this separately identified official connection. The public inventory therefore distinguishes an unavailable provider connection from an economy with no usable connection at all. SEC access failures and other unfilled gaps remain explicit.

An independent scheduled acquisition exposed a Yahoo classification change for TTF=F: the same NYMEX/EUR series is now labelled ALTSYMBOL. The adapter accepts that exact source label only for TTF=F on NYM, retains the provider's original label separately from the FUTURE contract class, and keeps currency/price/unit checks. No conversion or substitute ticker is used. The [provider page](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/TTF%3DF/) and [CME TTF contract notice](https://www.cmegroup.com/notices/ser/2017/06/SER-7929R.pdf) identify the calendar-month contract; distinct USD/MMBtu products are not used. Unknown symbols, exchanges, currencies or classifications still reject. Source coverage and chart details display the provider classification when it differs.

## Portfolio accounting and statement review

- Sourced split/reverse-split events change share quantity and average cost while preserving cash, total remaining cost and original transactions. Events retain their date and sequence. Duplicate same-date share-ratio events, ambiguous ordering, unsupported ratios and fractional settlement beyond ledger precision are rejected. No cash-in-lieu or corporate-action event is inferred. A published raw close is allowed after a known split only when the recorded date and ratio match. Later retrieval-vintage splits still block historical raw-price automation.
- Separate `Fee` and `Tax` cash flows reduce cash inside NAV. They are not external investor withdrawals. Gross dividends plus a separate tax, or net dividends alone, are supported recording choices; the UI warns against recording both treatments for one payment. Trade fees remain in trading cost/profit. Separately recorded expenses remain distinct from realized trading profit.
- Optional FX execution evidence retains original debit/receipt currencies, amounts, actual rate, received-currency fee, date and statement reference. Decimal integer arithmetic rounds the gross receipt half-up to six places before subtracting the explicitly recorded fee. The native deposit/withdrawal leg must match; no execution rate is inferred from public reference FX. This is one account's external cash leg, not cross-account transfer consolidation or a multi-currency cash ledger. Fees already deducted from the received amount must not be recorded again as a native expense.
- Closing-statement comparisons retain complete long-position quantities, cash, date and source. Exact symbols are used; omitted positions mean zero on that statement. Differences and unknown opening cash stay visible. Saving never creates balancing trades or cash adjustments. The economic ledger fingerprint identifies later edits within the comparison period; explanations alone do not invalidate it. A matching balance is a comparison against user-supplied evidence, not independent broker verification or NAV certification.

All optional fields are validated and included in existing record/complete/encrypted backups. No owner record is migrated or edited by this release.

## Prospective outcome review

The existing AR1/Ridge/Mean/Zero registry at 5/20 source-session horizons now reads one publication manifest and shares bounded history acquisitions across registrations. Checks validate listing, currency, source hash, acquisition vintage, ordered dates and target-price availability. Partial windows show observed session progress without inventing results. Completed settlement retains its source URL/hash and acquisition/publication dates in addition to its outcome prices and scores.

Later checks also review settled source windows. Changed anchors or outcome prices retain the original forecasts and original outcome, flag an unresolved source revision, and withhold the affected headline scores. A subsequent unavailable source does not clear that flag. Agreement with the original retained window resolves it. The latest 30 distinct source-review states are retained; repeated identical checks do not add another history entry. Concurrent changes are preserved instead of overwritten.

The view checks automatically at most hourly when opened; closed browsers do not run private registry checks. Device registration times and source hashes are not certified third-party timestamps. Real future outcomes must actually arrive before predictive accuracy can be measured. No additional advanced model is silently included and no trading profitability claim is made.
